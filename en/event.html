Hosting with Kwerk

All three of our Paris addresses are available for private hire. Our teams design and manage every event with the same care they bring to the spaces themselves, creating an atmosphere that feels genuinely like home. We also organise events beyond our own addresses.

The Rooftops

Three sky-high venues in the heart of the 8th arrondissement, with sweeping views over Paris's most iconic landmarks, from early morning to sunset.

Sundowner cocktails, dinner under the Parisian sky, a DJ set, a mixology workshop, a pop-up installation: every event is shaped around the atmosphere and rhythm you have in mind.

Restaurant Le Dana

A signature cuisine crafted by our executive chef, using French, seasonal and homemade produce.

Eighty covers: fifty inside, thirty on the quiet, open terrace. The restaurant accommodates up to one hundred guests for a standing cocktail reception and can be taken over exclusively for a seated dinner or an evening with a DJ.

Meeting Rooms

Around twenty rooms across our four addresses, from a two-person office to a 120-seat conference centre. All fully private, at every capacity: timber-framed rooms with skylights for keynotes, board sessions and press launches; more intimate spaces for strategy committees and private dinners.

All are run to the same standard as the rest of the house. Sound and visual production handled by our technical team. Catering available on request, from a welcome coffee to a full seated lunch. Bookable by the hour, half-day or on an exclusive long-term basis.

The Lobbies

Three lobbies, available for private hire in the evenings. High ceilings, designer furniture, bars staffed by our team. Up to 250 standing guests for a launch, an investor evening, a product reveal.

Furniture, tableware, sound, lighting: everything is in-house.
