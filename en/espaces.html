Offices designed and operated
to the standards of a great house

From intimate offices to entirely private floors, our Paris addresses primarily welcome teams from finance, technology and corporate law — clients for whom the quality of their address is part of their success.

EXECUTIVE
OFFICES

10 TO 60 M²

Beautifully appointed offices, fully furnished and filled with natural light, designed to welcome you in an elegant, serene setting. Each space combines comfort, discretion and confidentiality, offering optimal working conditions in an environment built for focus.

SIGNATURE
SUITES

40 TO 270 M²

Fully customisable suites combining reception areas, private lounges and meeting rooms. True spaces for representation, they offer a premium setting for meetings and for welcoming your guests, in an atmosphere both intimate and refined.

PRIVATE
FLOORS

FROM 150 M²

Entirely private floors, configurable to your specifications, incorporating reception areas, collaborative zones, call booths and private terraces. Each floor offers great freedom of layout, making it possible to create a working environment that reflects your company's identity and needs.
