Reception and concierge, dining, sport and wellness, events, maintenance, digital app: every service is operated to give you a unique experience.

RECEPTION & CONCIERGE

Our teams bring the art of personal service — inspired by the best Parisian hospitality houses — to every interaction. Consistent, attentive and warm, they support your colleagues and guests day after day, making every moment at the office feel effortless and memorable.

RECEPTION

  • Member and guest reception
  • Parcel and mail handling
  • Daily requests and enquiries

CONCIERGE

  • Reservation management
  • Professional travel coordination
  • Complex and bespoke requests

Our chefs and nutritionists design the full food offering — from semi-gastronomic dining to the bakery, from the brasserie to the barista counter. Every menu reflects the team, every dish follows the seasons, and everything is fresh and entirely house-made.

TRADITIONAL BRASSERIE

  • Generous, accessible everyday cooking

BAKERY & SANDWICH BAR

  • Breads, pastries and sandwiches prepared each morning
  • Available throughout the day, for every schedule

SMART FRIDGE

  • Fresh, accessible options
  • Ideal for colleagues on the go or working non-standard hours

BARISTA & TEA BAR

  • Specialty coffees, curated infusions, signature drinks and house-made snacks
