PRIVATE RESTAURANT

LE DANA — THE RESTAURANT

Discover Le Dana, our restaurant reserved for members and their guests. The menu, renewed weekly by our chef Laís Borba, celebrates authentic, refined cuisine crafted from seasonal, locally sourced ingredients — generous, precise and perfectly balanced.

COFFEE BREAKS & SIGNATURE DRINKS

LOUNGE & BARISTA

Spaces designed to welcome you throughout the day, offering specialty coffees, teas, fresh drinks and light bites at your own pace. Herbal tea stations on every floor complete the offering with infused waters, fresh fruit and seasonal infusions.

STUDIOS & COACHING

SPORT

Our sport programme brings together a wide range of disciplines — from HIIT and boxing to cycling, yoga, pilates and Hyrox. Over 40 hours of classes are offered each week, led by expert coaches. In addition, the gym facilities are freely accessible 24/7, so every member can train at their own pace and on their own terms.

EVERYDAY SERENITY

WELLNESS

We integrate daily meditation rituals and personalised Ayurvedic support to place well-being at the heart of everyday life. The experience extends into dedicated recovery and relaxation spaces: saunas, nap rooms and flotation cabins.
