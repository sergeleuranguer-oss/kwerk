MESSINE

Located on the prestigious Avenue de Messine, this Haussmann-style building offers fully customisable private offices, an elegant lobby, a dedicated barista service and two landscaped rooftops with panoramic views over Paris.

ADDRESS

  • 16 avenue de Messine, 75008 Paris
  • 4,300 sqm building
  • Car, motorcycle & bicycle parking

OUTDOOR SPACES

  • 2 panoramic 360° rooftops, up to 100 guests
  • 1 ground-floor patio

HOSPITALITY & SERVICES

  • 1,800 sqm of reception spaces
  • 1 large meeting room, up to 22 people
  • 3 sports studios & 5 private bathrooms
  • 2 infrared saunas
  • 1 flotation room
  • 1 nap room
  • 1 specialty coffee barista
  • 1 bistro (lunch space, private hire, multiple formats)

METRO
