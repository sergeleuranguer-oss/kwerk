YOUR DETAILS

Kwerk welcomes you across its three signature addresses

At the heart of Paris's central business district, in the 8th arrondissement, where the elegance of the lobbies, the attention given to services and the beauty of the panoramic rooftops redefine the art of hospitality.

16 AVENUE DE MESSINE, 75008 PARIS

MESSINE — A HERITAGE ELEVATED

The story of the place
The building carries a history. In the 19th century, it belonged to a family of tapestry makers, a legacy that continues to inhabit the spaces today.
The fabric dome
In the lobby, a fabric dome suspended above the light well creates a floating, aerial presence. The textile mouldings compose an almost immaterial sculpture, at once a tribute to this artisanal past and a contemporary architectural statement.
A dialogue of contrasts
Set against this lightness, massive stone blocks cradle carved seats with generous curves. The opposition is deliberate: heavy and light, mineral and textile, the immutable and the ephemeral.

18 RUE DE COURCELLES, 75008 PARIS

SAINT-HONORÉ — THE DREAMLIKE FOREST

The bamboo canopy
Enter, and the space speaks for itself: a bamboo canopy soaring over ten metres. Sourced from a forest in Osaka, the bamboo is treated using an ancestral Japanese technique that gives it a deep, warm tone. A singular piece, at once raw and masterfully crafted.
The ceiling: a surface in motion
In the lobby, a suspended latex ceiling undulates gently. A living material, it restores a sense of verticality to the space, transforming the volume into a forest vault and dissolving familiar spatial markers.
An immersive world
Every detail here evokes a reimagined forest. Terrariums and planted features structure the spaces like natural undergrowth. The furniture, with its floral and vivid accents, contrasts with the raw material of the bamboo. Each element reveals the hand of the craftsman: visible seams, deliberate joints, honest finishes.

22 BOULEVARD MALESHERBES, 75008 PARIS

MADELEINE — THE TEMPLE OF WORK

A place apart
Only a few steps from the church of La Madeleine. A place to work, but also to slow down and find a certain stillness.
A language of its own
At the heart of the lobby, an alphabet of 22 symbols unfolds. Hand-carved in volcanic stone by skilled artisans, each character etched into the walls, a quiet counterpoint to the Haussmannian stone outside.
A light that evolves
A circadian totem in backlit stone accompanies the day. Its light shifts from morning to evening, brighter at the start and softer as the day draws to a close, following the body's natural rhythm.
The barista, at the heart of the space
This is where Kwerk's barista offering takes shape. Stationed behind a wall of hand-shaped salt bricks from Indonesia, the barista is central to the atmosphere of the space, simple and quietly calming.
