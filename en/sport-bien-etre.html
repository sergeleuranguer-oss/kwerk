Sport and wellness
as part of the balance of our spaces

Sports studios, group or individual sessions with our expert coaches, recovery spaces and moments for yourself — all contributing to the balance of the Kwerk experience, every day.

STUDIOS & COACHING

Our sport programme brings together a wide range of disciplines, with over 40 hours of classes offered each week, led by our team of expert coaches. In addition, the gym facilities are freely accessible 24/7, so every member can train at their own pace and on their own terms.

SPORT

  • Boxing (English & Thai)
  • HIIT
  • Cycling
  • Hyrox Preparation
  • Strength Training
  • Cardio, Cross & Functional Training
  • Run Club & Athletic Running
  • Classic, Strength & Flow Pilates
  • Vinyasa & Iyengar Yoga

EVERYDAY WELLNESS

We have developed a holistic approach to wellness, centred on balance, rest and breathwork. Tailored programmes are available, incorporating meditation, personalised Ayurvedic guidance and nutritional advice developed in collaboration with our chefs. This approach is supported by dedicated spaces and equipment designed for wellness and recovery — spaces conceived to help every member take a moment to recentre and care for themselves throughout the day.

WELLNESS

  • Sauna
  • Flotation cabin
  • Nap room
  • Meditation programme
  • Personal coaching
  • Nutritionist
