YOUR DETAILS

Your building,
operated like a Kwerk address

For over ten years, Kwerk has developed an operator's expertise across its own Parisian addresses — expertise tested daily by the companies who work within them. This practice, refined in close contact with your teams, now enables us to bring the hospitality standards of our addresses directly into your offices.

Tenants, owners, asset managers: we take full responsibility for every aspect of your address, so that each detail contributes to an experience that is uniquely yours.

One offer, two perspectives

OWNERS & ASSET MANAGERS

Enhance the value of your real estate asset

A building operated by Kwerk stands out in its market. It attracts discerning tenants and retains them for longer. Our role: to elevate your asset to the hospitality standard of our own Parisian addresses.

OCCUPYING COMPANIES

Your headquarters, experienced as a Kwerk address.

For your talent and your guests alike, Kwerk transforms your headquarters into a place of genuine appeal. Our teams anticipate the needs of daily life, and the standard of service mirrors that of our own addresses.

HOSPITALITY

Our teams cultivate the art of personalised service, drawing inspiration from the codes of the world's finest hotels. They support your employees and their guests each day, turning every moment at the office into an experience that meets your highest expectations.

RECEPTION

  • Welcoming employees and their guests
  • Handling parcels and mail
  • Dedicated point of contact for day-to-day requests
  • International press available on-site

CONCIERGE

  • Reservations (restaurants, hotels, taxis, tickets, travel)
  • Organisation of business travel
  • Personal day-to-day services (dry cleaning, errands, deliveries)
  • Handling complex and bespoke requests

EXPERIENCE

  • Animation of shared spaces
  • Communications and events programming for the address
  • Signature sound and scent atmospheres
  • Signage, décor and greenery

MAINTENANCE & FACILITIES

  • Daily eco-responsible cleaning
  • Preventive and corrective technical maintenance
  • Dedicated building manager and facilities supervision
  • Activity reports and personalised follow-up

DINING

A table is the heart of any house. At Kwerk, our chefs and nutritionist work together to create fresh, seasonal, entirely house-made cuisine. Each address has its own format, each team its own rhythm: from the morning coffee to the eagerly anticipated lunch, from the everyday restaurant to more signature formats for your key meetings. Accessible and honest cooking that knows how to be generous when the moment calls for it.

STAFF RESTAURANT

  • Generous, everyday cooking accessible to all
  • A menu that changes daily — hot and cold, familiar and adventurous — balanced with input from our nutritionist
  • A well-paced service, designed for the midday break

EXECUTIVE BRASSERIE

  • A chef's table dedicated to business lunches
  • Attentive plated service and a short menu showcasing the sourcing of each ingredient

ALTERNATIVE DINING

  • Formats with a clear identity: corner, counter, permanent food truck
  • Extended opening hours for quick and easy consumption

BARISTA & TEA BAR

  • Specialty coffees
  • House-made plant-based drinks, prepared to your taste
  • Fresh drinks made to order
  • House-made sweet and savoury snacks

EVENTS CATERING

  • From coffee breaks to cocktail dinners
  • Prestigious lunches and dinners orchestrated by our chefs
  • Access to an exclusive selection of Kwerk and partner venues
