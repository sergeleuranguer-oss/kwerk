A certain idea of the Parisian office,
an intuition turned into a craft

An office is not merely a place to work, but an address where you feel at home. A place where you are received as in a house you love.

The Kwerk story

Albert Angel & Lawrence Knights

Design architect & entrepreneur

Albert Angel is an architect. A 1996 graduate from Cape Town, he moved to New York, where he joined Ralph Appelbaum Associates and spent a decade designing museums — spaces created to stir emotion in everyone who walks through them.

In 2008, in New York, his practice had its offices in what would become the very first WeWork. It was there that he crossed paths with its founder, Adam Neumann, and met Lawrence Knights. From this twofold encounter came a simple intuition: to bring the codes of hospitality into the workplace.

Seven years later, in 2015, they opened the first Kwerk address in Paris. Their conviction can be summed up in a single sentence: to run an office the way one runs a grand Parisian house. A place that is chosen, signed for, and operated over the long term.

Today, Kwerk has three addresses in the 8th arrondissement — three unique pieces from a single collection. Every service is run by our own teams, from morning to night.

A philosophy built more than ten years ago, blending design, sport, nutrition and rituals — all in service of effectiveness at work.

The faces of Kwerk

Aurélien Bialek

Director of Hospitality and Guest Experience

Fifteen years of experience leading reception and service teams in the finest hotels, notably at Cheval Blanc Paris and Le Bristol.

At Kwerk, he brings the exacting standards of luxury hospitality to the workplace. He trains and supports the front-of-house teams day to day, so that every visitor is received with the same care and every visit to our addresses is experienced as an act of hospitality.

Maria Ushakova

Interior architect

Travel has, to this day, been one of the main sources of inspiration for Maria's work. Having lived in different parts of the world has nurtured her taste for eclectic interiors, blending classic and contemporary pieces, singular works of art, vintage furniture and antiques.

A partner of Kwerk, she specialises in creating living spaces and seeks above all to design places that are balanced, comfortable, harmonious and elegant — interiors that tell a unique story and offer their occupants a true experience of living.

Vincent Cosme

Executive chef

More than fifteen years of experience in fine dining, having worked in houses such as the Ritz-Carlton and Relais & Châteaux establishments. Vincent also spent six years as a private chef and delivered high-end event catering. An award-winning chef, he was named Champion de France Traiteur (French Catering Champion) in 2021.

At Kwerk, he creates a dining experience that extends the spirit of the house: honest, generous, produce-led cooking. Here, every lunch becomes a highlight of the day.

Lizette Scialom

Nutritionist

Specialising in functional nutrition, with more than ten years of experience using diet as a lever for vitality and performance, Lizette approaches nutrition by combining scientific rigour, microbiome balance and an understanding of physiological needs.

A partner of Kwerk, she reviews and enriches our menus to ensure dishes that are at once flavourful, balanced and rich in nutritional value.

Charlotte Tarbouriech

Sport & wellness expert

Fifteen years of experience as a designer for major fashion houses. Co-founder of POLCHA, a creative studio specialising in object, furniture and interior design. She also co-founded Elevate Training Hotel, a hospitality project built around sport and wellbeing.

A partner of Kwerk, she brings the eye of both a designer and an athlete to wellbeing at the office. She designs the spaces, rituals and programmes that make room for the body throughout the working day.

“We created Kwerk because we wanted an office we would have dreamed of working in.”
Albert Angel — Co-founder
